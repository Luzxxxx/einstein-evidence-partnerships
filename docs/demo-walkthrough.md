# Two-minute demo

1. Open the [public demo](https://einstein-evidence-partnerships.clearbread14.chatgpt.site). Three cards correspond to three different approved company/researcher pairs.
2. Open FreshPak. Read the partner rationale, the bounded pilot and the approval conditions in Overview.
3. Open a source citation. Inspect the original field, exact excerpt and source identity. Close the evidence drawer.
4. Open Review, then Activity. The saved trace separates Proposer, Counter-evidence and Critic activity.
5. Open History and the ArmaturaSec research run ending in **Insufficient evidence**. Review the two drafts, two Critic reviews and Changes. The product preserves uncertainty instead of forcing a match.
6. Optionally inspect the separately labelled adversarial diagnostic to see a revise → v2 → approve chain. It is not one of the formal three proposals.
7. Use the README commands to run locally with your own API. The public demo is saved playback and makes no new model requests.
