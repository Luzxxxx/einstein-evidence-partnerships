C105 / R0123

Challenge C105-CH2 · approved draft version 1

summary

R0123 is a strong technical match for C105-CH2: a bioinformatics/HPC researcher with registered evidence on cloud cost modelling for alignment and variant-calling pipelines, Snakemake scatter–gather throughput improvement with concordance checks, and a documented industry optimisation engagement, aligned with the company's unprofiled but suspected alignment/calling bottlenecks.

Claims: C1, C3, C4, C5, C6

technicalRationale

The evidence covers the two likely levers: (a) cost-aware execution on spot/preemptible instances with checkpointing, and (b) parallelism/scatter–gather restructuring of alignment and calling stages, both with explicit accuracy-concordance measurements rather than speed alone.

Claims: C3, C4, C6

Project GENOMFLO develops a cost-aware orchestration layer and containerised alignment/variant-calling components for laboratories with limited bioinformatics staff, which resembles the company's two-person bioinformatics setup.

Claims: C2, C6

cooperationScope

A time-boxed pilot (roughly 13 weeks) under contract research or consulting: a 4-week profiling and cost-model phase, a 4-week optimisation of alignment/calling parallelism and checkpointed spot execution, and a 4-week concordance validation and handover to the company's two bioinformatics staff.

Claims: C7, C8, C10

validation

Acceptance gates: per-rule profile before/after, compute cost per sample measured against provider billing, total batch wall time, and star-allele call concordance versus the current pipeline on retained ethics-approved samples, with clinical accuracy treated as a non-negotiable constraint.

Claims: C7, C8

Concordance methodology should follow the cited practice of comparing against the existing baseline with precision/recall style metrics, not just timing.

Claims: C4, C8

constraints

Reported cost and runtime gains derive from WGS/WES workloads and modelled spot markets; they are not guarantees for a targeted star-allele panel, and spot usage without checkpointing can increase effective cost.

Claims: C3, C4, C8

Collaboration preferences support contract research, consulting and joint projects; availability, fees, IP and data-processing agreements are not documented and must be agreed separately.

Claims: C10

unknowns

The company's actual per-step bottlenecks, instance types and billing detail are unmeasured, so the sub-3-EUR and under-90-minute targets remain untested hypotheses.

Claims: C6, C7

Identity of co-authors and co-participants on the cited records is partly unresolved in the source data; no conclusions are drawn about their roles.

Claims: C9

C3 (fact)

R0123 co-authored work building a cost model for WGS alignment and variant-calling pipelines that combines hourly spot price distributions from two cloud providers, mean time to interruption, and checkpoint overhead for BWA-MEM2 and GATK stages; naive spot use without checkpointing increased effective cost 18–34% in high-volatility markets, whereas a bidding-and-checkpoint strategy cut cost 41% on average versus on-demand in stable price periods.

  • Modelled on WGS/WES cohorts at 10×–30× coverage, not on targeted short-read star-allele panels; transfer to a 24-sample panel batch is an assumption.
  • Pricing sampled over 90 days from two providers; results are sensitive to market volatility.

C4 (fact)

R0123 co-authored a study on Snakemake/SLURM orchestration for GATK HaplotypeCaller, where genomic-interval scatter–gather reduced mean per-sample runtime from 4.1 hours to 41 minutes on 32 cores, with SNP precision 99.91%/recall 99.87% and indel precision 99.34%/recall 99.21% against the baseline on GIAB variants.

  • WES cohort at mean 85× coverage; the accuracy comparison is concordance with the authors' own baseline, not an independent clinical validation.

C8 (proposal)

Targeted optimisation phase using interval scatter–gather and sample-level parallelism for the alignment and calling stages, plus checkpointed spot/preemptible execution guided by the cost model, with concordance against the current calls measured on retained clinical samples as the accuracy gate for the under-90-minute and sub-3-EUR goals.

  • Runtime and cost reductions reported in the cited studies were obtained on WGS/WES workloads, not on a targeted star-allele panel; gains may be smaller.
  • Effort assumes a 13-week pilot at the modest pace the proposer assigns, i.e. a 4-week plan, monitoring and a 4-week optimisation phase, with a 4-week accuracy-validation and handover period.
  • Spot-instance strategies carry interruption risk and require checkpointing to avoid cost regressions.
